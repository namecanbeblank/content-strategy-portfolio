# Content strategy and editorial systems

I build AI-assisted workflows that help content teams turn research into focused briefs and stronger drafts. This collection shows the kinds of problems the workflows address and the outputs a team can use.

## Research and strategy

### Specificity profile
Defines the intended reader and the situation behind a buying decision, giving a content team a sharper foundation for its argument.

**Output:** A reader and problem profile for an article or campaign.

### Search intent analysis
Examines search results to understand the different jobs readers may be trying to complete, helping a team decide which audience and intent a page should serve.

**Output:** An intent assessment and page-direction recommendation.

### Information-gain analysis
Reviews the existing search landscape to find where a client has a credible opportunity to contribute useful, differentiated information.

**Output:** An evidence-informed map of coverage and potential opportunities.

### Customer evidence analysis
Turns interviews, reviews, sales conversations, and other customer language into insights a content team can use to build a more relevant argument.

**Output:** Research themes, supporting evidence, and a quote bank.

### Article positioning
Connects a specific buyer problem with the product's relevant strengths and the alternatives a reader is considering.

**Output:** A positioning direction for a particular article.

## Briefing and editorial production

### Directional content brief
Packages the intended outcome, argument, supporting evidence, and constraints into a usable handoff for a writer.

**Output:** A strategy brief that gives the writer clear direction.

### BOFU content rewriting
Improves bottom-of-funnel SaaS drafts so the buyer problem, product explanation, and article argument are more specific and useful.

**Output:** A revised draft grounded in the supplied product and research material.

## Decisions and interpretation

### Strategic interpretation
Connects audience and product evidence to patterns and messaging direction while preserving where the evidence came from.

**Output:** A documented interpretation for positioning or briefing.

### Decision closure
Helps a team decide when it has enough information to act and when a specific unanswered question warrants further investigation.

**Output:** A decision record with the rationale and remaining uncertainties.

These summaries describe capabilities and deliverables. The underlying instructions and supporting materials are not included in this portfolio. Outputs depend on the inputs available and are reviewed by a human.
